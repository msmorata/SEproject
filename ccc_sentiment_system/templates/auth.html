Republic Act No. 10173

Institutional Data Privacy Notice & Informed Consent

In compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations, City College of Calamba (CCC) commits to protecting your personal and academic information.

1. Purpose of Data Collection

Your institutional username, email address, role, and enrolled program in the Department of Computing and Informatics (DCI) are collected strictly to verify your identity as a bona fide member of the CCC academic community and prevent duplicate or unauthorized survey submissions.

2. Anonymization & AI Sentiment Processing

  • Strict Decoupling: When you submit survey evaluations or Town Hall feedback, your narrative text is permanently separated from your account identity before analysis.
  • Automated PII Scrubbing: Phone numbers, email addresses, student ID numbers, and self-introductions are automatically masked prior to database storage.
  • Small-Group Suppression (k-Anonymity): Survey results with fewer than 5 respondents are suppressed from non-MISD views so individual students cannot be singled out.
  • Coarsened Timestamps: Submission timestamps are rounded to prevent timing-correlation attacks.

3. Data Retention & Your Rights Under RA 10173

Aggregated sentiment records are retained solely for institutional quality assurance and academic research evaluation. As a data subject, you have the right to be informed, the right to object, the right to access, and the right to request account deletion or blocking through the MISD office.
